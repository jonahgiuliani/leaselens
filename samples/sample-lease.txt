RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of December 1, 2026, by and between
Telegraph Property Group LLC ("Landlord") and the undersigned tenant(s) ("Tenant"),
for the premises located at 2455 Telegraph Avenue, Unit 3B, Berkeley, CA 94704 ("Premises").

1. TERM. The term of this Lease shall be twelve (12) months, beginning January 1, 2027
and ending December 31, 2027. The Lease shall automatically renew for successive
twelve-month terms unless Tenant provides written notice of non-renewal at least
ninety (90) days prior to the end of the term.

2. RENT. Monthly rent is Two Thousand Four Hundred Dollars ($2,400.00), due on the
first day of each month. Rent received after the 3rd day of the month shall incur a
late fee of One Hundred Fifty Dollars ($150.00) plus Twenty-Five Dollars ($25.00) per
additional day until paid in full.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to three (3) months'
rent ($7,200.00), due at signing. A Four Hundred Dollar ($400.00) non-refundable
cleaning fee shall be deducted from the security deposit at move-out.

4. CONDITION OF PREMISES; REPAIRS. Tenant accepts the Premises "as is" and waives all
claims relating to habitability or condition. Tenant is responsible for all repairs
and maintenance to the Premises during the term, including without limitation
plumbing, heating, electrical systems, and appliances.

5. ENTRY BY LANDLORD. Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at any
time, with or without notice, to inspect, repair, or show the unit to prospective
tenants or buyers.

6. GUESTS. Guests staying more than two (2) consecutive nights shall incur a charge
of Fifty Dollars ($50.00) per night, payable with the next month's rent.

7. SUBLETTING. Subletting, assignment, or licensing of the Premises, in whole or in
part, including through any short-term rental platform, is strictly prohibited.

8. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, Tenant shall pay
as liquidated damages an amount equal to four (4) months' rent, plus all costs of
reletting, and Landlord shall have no obligation to mitigate damages.

9. LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any injury, loss, or damage to persons
or property occurring on the Premises from any cause, including Landlord's own
negligence, and Tenant waives all such claims.

10. UTILITIES. Tenant shall pay all utilities, including water, gas, electric, trash,
and a monthly building amenities fee of $75.00.

11. INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain renters insurance with liability coverage of at
least $100,000 naming Landlord as an additional insured, with proof delivered within
ten (10) days of the start of the term.

12. PETS. No pets of any kind are permitted. Violation of this section incurs a fee
of $500.00 per occurrence plus immediate termination rights for Landlord.

13. QUIET HOURS. Quiet hours are 9:00 PM to 8:00 AM daily. Repeated violations may be
treated as a material breach.

14. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord shall be sent to: Telegraph Property Group LLC,
PO Box 7421, Berkeley, CA 94707.

TENANT ACKNOWLEDGES THAT TENANT HAS READ AND UNDERSTANDS ALL TERMS OF THIS LEASE.
